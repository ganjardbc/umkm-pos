## Summary

WisataPOS (repo: UMKM POS) is a multi-tenant Point of Sale SaaS for tourism-sector small businesses (UMKM)
in Indonesia: one merchant account manages many outlets, with POS transactions, stock, cashier shifts, daily
reports, and customer self-ordering through a table QR code.

## Problem

From "Core Value Proposition" in `docs/product/requirements.md`: tourism UMKM need a POS that is fast and
still usable offline, stock that updates automatically from every transaction, several outlets under one
account, cashier access limited to their own outlet, and simple daily reports.

The source document contains no user research or problem data — an open question for the Product team.

## Target Users

- **Segment:** cafés, souvenir shops, agro-tourism, small multi-booth merchants.
- **Merchant Owner:** manages outlets, users and roles, products; sees reports for all outlets.
- **Cashier:** opens/closes shifts, processes transactions, sees their shift history.
- **Customer (self-order):** scans the table QR, browses the menu, orders — no login.
- **Platform admin:** manages merchants through `apps/admin` (present in the code, not yet in the
  requirements document).

## Scope

In scope (modules in `docs/product/requirements.md`): authentication, merchant, outlets, users + per-outlet
RBAC, products + categories, POS terminal, shifts (multi-cashier), stock, transaction history, daily reports,
QR self-order, tables, in-app notifications.

Out of scope (MVP Non-Goals): loyalty/points, CRM, supplier & purchasing, multi-warehouse, tax engine,
accounting, WhatsApp/email integration, custom domain, white label, marketplace.

Note: the requirements mention an offline mode with sync; whether it exists in the code was not verified for
this document.

## Success Criteria

There are no business metrics in the repo. **Proposed by Claude, not confirmed** — derived from "Technical
Constraints" in the requirements, so each one is checkable:

- No merchant's data can be read by another merchant (every query scoped by `merchant_id` from the JWT).
- Transaction commit is atomic: the transaction, its items, the stock change, and the logs are all saved or
  none are.
- Prices in `transaction_items` do not change when a product's price changes.
- A cashier can only access outlets where they hold a role.

Adoption/business metrics (number of merchants, transactions per day, etc.) — an open question for the
Product team.
