Insell Design System

Foundations and components for insell.id — marketing site, social content, and the POS dashboard. Cream-based light surfaces, primary-field marketing contexts, and a navy-derived dark mode, all from one token set.

Colors

Primary ramp (brand-fixed anchors), neutral ramp cream→navy, semantic accents, and the three usage contexts.

Typography

Manrope for display/headings, Inter for body, UI, and tabular data.

Iconography

Lucide (CDN substitution — flagged, no icon set was supplied).

Layout & grid

4px spacing base, radius scale, 12-col grid.

Elevation

Navy-tinted shadows, five steps plus a brand-tinted variant.

Motion

Fast, purposeful transitions; spring reserved for confirmations.

Image treatment

Warm, natural product photography. Striped placeholders until real assets land.

Logo usage

Clear space, minimum size, on-ground knockout.

Dark mode

Navy-derived surfaces and text — not generic gray.
