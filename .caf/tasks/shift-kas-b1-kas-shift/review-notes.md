## Review Notes — shift-kas-b1-kas-shift
Ticket: shift-kas-b1-kas-shift
Agent: caf-reviewer
Verdict: CHANGES REQUESTED

### Security Audit
- merchant scoping holds. `closeShift` still resolves the merchant's outlet ids and filters `shifts` by them (apps/api/src/shifts/shifts.service.ts). `merchant_id` comes from `@CurrentUser`. The controller keeps the existing `@RequirePermission` codes and adds no new permission.
- Low: `payTransaction` accepts a client `shift_id`. It checks that the shift is open and that the caller is an active participant. It does not check that the shift belongs to the transaction's outlet or merchant (`resolveCashierForPos`, transactions.service.ts:768). The participant check limits this, but a cashier in a shift at another outlet could route cash to it. The implementer flagged the same gap. Needs a human decision on whether to add an outlet match.
- Low: `closeShift` sums sales inside a `$transaction`, but `payTransaction` validates the open shift outside any transaction. A cash payment in flight when the shift closes could be credited to `paid_shift_id` after `expected_cash` is already stored. The effect is a wrong expected cash. This is a race window and was not tested.

### Verdict Rationale

1. Four ACs (AC-12..AC-15) have only manual evidence that was not run. They count as needs human check, not passed.
